About Minecraft

What is Minecraft?

Minecraft is a popular 3D sandbox video game developed by Mojang Studios and officially released in 2011.

Players can explore a virtual world, collect resources, build houses, craft tools, fight enemies and create amazing structures.

Minecraft encourages creativity, imagination, teamwork and problem-solving.

Game Modes

Explore maps and complete exciting challenges.
